Eurise brings your everyday apps together in one light app: a home screen (launcher), Today, Calendar, Tasks, Notes, Contacts, Clock, Files, Scanner and PDF reader.

<b>Everything stays on your phone.</b> No trackers, no analytics. You decide what leaves the device, with a switch and two lines that say what is read, where it goes and how to undo it. The Privacy center shows where every piece of data lives.

<b>The apps talk to each other.</b> An event knows the meeting note, the people and the files that belong to it. A quick sentence becomes an event, a task or a note.

<b>Europe.</b> Connects to European clouds such as Murena, kDrive or your own Nextcloud in two taps: no keys, no technical addresses.

<b>Light.</b> The app weighs a few megabytes; heavy content is downloaded only when needed.

Open source, GPLv3. The other apps of the suite (Password, Gallery, Keyboard, Voice) are separate APKs and talk to Eurise only when signed with the same key.
